About

I'm a graduate researcher in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, working at the intersection of reinforcement learning, multimodal learning, and agentic AI. My goal is to develop AI that can reliably reason and generalize in complex, real-world environments.

I currently train large vision-language models that learn when and how to retrieve external knowledge and self-verify their answers, and I build agentic pipelines that connect perception, planning, and human-in-the-loop reasoning. Earlier work spans spatial machine learning, computer vision, and full-stack development.

Featured projects

Featured · Live demo below

RepSC-SOM — Spatial Regionalization

A novel spatially-constrained self-organizing map with representative initialization, adaptive geographic filtering, and region-growing refinement. Applied to flood-induced E. coli contamination in Jacksonville, it detects hotspots more accurately than census tracts and standard units. Published in Scientific Reports (Nature).

PythonSOMGeospatial MLClustering
▶ Try the live demo GitHub ↗

Multimodal VQA with RL & Agentic Retrieval

A vision-language model trained with GRPO to reason over images, decide when to retrieve external knowledge, and self-verify answers. Multi-turn agentic pipeline with FAISS dense retrieval over 21M Wikipedia passages, on 4× B200 GPUs (FSDP · vLLM · Ray).

PyTorchGRPOvLLMFAISSRay

Agentic AI Planning Support System

An agentic framework integrating LLM-based reasoning, planning, and human-in-the-loop interaction to generate demand-oriented regionalization for disaster adaptation — built on top of RepSC-SOM with natural-language constraint exploration.

LLMsAgentic AIHuman-in-the-loop

Campus Vulnerability — Flood Object Detection

An object detection system that identifies and classifies flood-prone architectural features from satellite & street-view imagery for campus vulnerability assessment.

OpenCVObject DetectionVLMs

Facial Trait & Multimodal Sentiment

Deep models predicting 42 psychological attributes from facial images with a novel activation function, plus a multimodal sentiment system combining facial analysis with Twitter text.
